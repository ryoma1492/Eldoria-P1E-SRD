# Fool’s Curtain

A vast, permanent, unnatural fog/barrier that appeared after the Great Dragon Wars roughly one hundred years ago. It hangs perpetually above and surrounding the island of Emberfall (and extends far beyond the immediate coastline in places).

While it effectively shields the island from external threats, the diminished sunlight has caused widespread agricultural collapse, especially in former Eldoria.

---

## Structure – The Three Layers

Pierre’s research (and later the methods of the [[Curtain  Splitter]] of [[Zarthas]]) established that the Curtain consists of three distinct layers:

### First Layer
Neutralized by paste made from [[Voidcap]] mushrooms.

### Second Layer
Also neutralized by [[Voidcap]] paste.

### Third Layer – The Malice Layer
- Pure, oppressive magical malice
- Instantly kills all non-sentient living things (plants, mindless organisms)
- For sentient beings, survival duration is directly tied to willpower / resolve
- Higher determination allows longer survival; a single stray thought or moment of weakness can be fatal

This third layer is the reason almost no one who attempts a full crossing returns intact.

---

## Known Interactions

- **[[Curtain  Splitter]]**: Magical-technological devices created by [[Zarthas]] that open temporary half-mile holes through the Curtain (primarily by addressing the first two layers).  
- **[[Voidcap]]**: The only known substance that reliably neutralizes the first two layers. Essential both for Splitters and for any attempted personal crossing.  
- **Funeral barges**: Some coastal cultures treat the Curtain as a sacred passage into death and willingly send the elderly or dying into it.  
- **[[(Pierre) - Piero Fruosino di Antonio da Vinci]]**: The only documented individual known to have partially survived a full crossing of all three layers. The cost was catastrophic (accelerated aging, magical perforations, soul-saturated malice).

---

## Cultural & Political Impact

- Created the “Fogborn” underclass in Eldoria.
- Forced mass migration and economic upheaval.
- Allowed the [[Royal Crown of Eldoria]] to maintain a permanent state of emergency taxation.
- Became the central obsession of both [[Zarthas]] (who seek to pierce or control it) and various cults.
