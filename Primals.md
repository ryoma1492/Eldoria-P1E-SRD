# Primals

Creatures (animal or plant) burdened by an excess of magical essence — sometimes described as being “doubled.”

Left unchecked, this overload eventually consumes or destabilizes them. Through the ancient [[Primal Bond]] ritual of the Kin, portions of that excess can be shared with humanoid hosts, stabilizing the Primal while granting the bonded individual survival adaptations.

In the [[Savage Lands]], Primals are far more common and far more powerful than ordinary wildlife because of the continual leak of [[Primal Energy]] from the wounded [[Gold Dragon]].

Examples:
- A Primal bear possesses the instincts and strength of several ordinary bears.
- A Primal hyena possesses multiple times the aggression, territorial instinct, and endurance of a normal hyena.

Most medium and large Primals are naturally volatile simply because aggression exists somewhere within their natural instincts. This is simply how nature works inside the Savage Lands.
