**Description** A single enormous, razor-sharpened black claw broken from Malazar himself. It was left embedded in the gold dragon’s wound and animates with residual malice and leaking primal energy the moment anyone attempts to heal or close the gash. Its sole purpose is to keep the wound open.

**Special Abilities**

- **Reopen the Wound (Su)** On a hit the target takes 1d6 persistent bleed. While within 60 ft. of the gold dragon’s wound this bleed is harder to stop (Heal DC 20 or caster level check DC 20).
- **Malice Pulse (Su)** Once every 1d3 rounds as a swift action. 15-ft. burst: 4d6 acid damage + sickened 1 round (Fort DC 17 half damage and negates sickened). Briefly intensifies the local leak (narrative only).
- **Wound-Tethered (Ex)** Cannot move more than 80 ft. from the gold dragon’s wound. If forced farther it is yanked back adjacent to the wound at the start of its next turn.
- **Malazar’s Signature (Su)** Against any creature carrying fragments of Malazar’s corruption (via Killian’s curse): +2 on attack and damage rolls, critical threat range becomes 18–20.
- **Residual Malice (Su)** Fast healing 4 while the main wound remains open. Suppressed if the party makes meaningful progress toward beginning to close the wound.
