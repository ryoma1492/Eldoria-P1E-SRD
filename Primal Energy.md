# Primal Energy

Raw life-shaping magical essence that continually leaks from the wounded [[Gold Dragon]].

It is the true source of:
- the extraordinary abundance of [[Primals]]
- the heightened vitality and aggression of wildlife in the [[Savage Lands]]
- primal plants
- the magical saturation felt throughout [[Savage Spire]]

When the obstruction ([[Malazar]]’s claw) is removed, the dragon will still require a living “seed” of unbound primal energy before the wound can fully seal. The voluntary surrender of existing [[Primal Bonds]] by the Kin of [[Savage Spire]] supplies that seed.

Once the leak ends, the [[Savage Lands]] begin a long, gradual return toward ecological balance. New uncontrolled Primals cease to form.
