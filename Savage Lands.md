# Savage Lands

A vast, primal wilderness region beyond the more settled coasts of Emberfall. Characterized by:

- Extreme abundance of [[Primals]] (both animal and plant)
- Oversized, aggressive wildlife
- Dense jungle and mesa terrain
- Overwhelming magical vitality

The true source of this heightened state is a continual leak of raw [[Primal Energy]] from a wounded ancient [[Gold Dragon]] deeper inland. The leak was caused by [[Malazar]], who left one of his own claws embedded in the dragon’s underbelly.

---

## Key Locations

- [[Savage Spire]] — the primary Kin settlement and the surface expression of the primal leak
- The inland region surrounding the [[Gold Dragon]] — where [[Pierre]] is rooted
- Various hunting grounds and migratory paths used by the Kin

---

## Cultural Notes

The Kin (animal-bonded humans) of the region have adapted to live alongside Primals rather than dominate them. Survival requires understanding the amplified instincts of the local wildlife.

Herd movements have grown more frequent and larger in recent times — a direct symptom of the intensifying leak.
