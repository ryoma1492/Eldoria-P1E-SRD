# Primal Bond

An ancient ritual practiced by the Kin of the [[Savage Spire]] and the wider [[Savage Lands]].

Primals are creatures (animal or plant) burdened by an excess of magical essence — sometimes described as being “doubled.” Left unchecked, this overload eventually consumes or destabilizes them.

Through a carefully controlled transfer older than living memory, portions of that excess essence can be shared with a humanoid host. The result:

- Stabilizes the Primal
- Grants the bonded humanoid survival adaptations suited to the hostile environment
- Creates the Kin (animal- or plant-bonded humans)

The relationship is treated as mutual survival, not domination or ownership.

---

## Limitations

- Essence can only be transferred into humanoid recipients.
- Existing Primals cannot meaningfully stack or exchange different primal states.
- Dragon essence is considered too volatile and is never used within the Spire system (it is too closely tied to [[Malazar]]).

---

## Narrative Role

The people of [[Savage Spire]] will ultimately be asked to voluntarily surrender their own Primal Bonds. The released energy returns to the wounded [[Gold Dragon]] and becomes the living “seed” required to finally close its wound.

To the Kin this is not viewed as loss of power. It is the return of a sacred gift to its rightful source.
