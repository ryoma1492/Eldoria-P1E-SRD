# Jellybean (Wings)

### Phase 1

**Passive: Featherstep**

- Ignore first 20 ft of falling damage.
- +2 Acrobatics.

**Active: Predator's Burst**  
Swift action.  
Move up to 10 ft without provoking.  
If this creates flanking, gain +1d6 sneak attack this round.

---

### Phase 2

**Passive: Drifting Wings**

- Always land on feet.
- Can glide 30 ft for every 10 ft descended.

**Active: Shadow Dive**  
Immediate action.  
Move 15 ft after being targeted by an attack.

---

### Phase 3

**Passive: True Wings**

- Fly 30 ft (average).

**Active: Murder Drop**  
After descending at least 10 ft:

- Next attack gains +2 attack.
- Double sneak attack dice.

---

# Shared Breath Progression

This is where I'd make the progression cumulative.

### Phase 1

**Acid Breath**  
15-ft line  
2d6 acid  
1/day

---

### Phase 2

Gain:

- 1 additional daily use
- Breath becomes 30 ft

Now:  
30-ft line  
2d6 acid  
2/day

---

### Phase 3

Gain:

- Damage increases to 4d6
- Creatures damaged become sickened 1 round

Final:  
30-ft line  
4d6 acid  
2/day  
+sickened

Notice this is intentionally _weaker than a real dragon_.
