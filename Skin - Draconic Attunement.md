A portion of the power of [[Killian’s Curse|Killian's Curse]] shared among the [[Heroes]] outside the [[Fool’s Curtain|Fool's Curtain]]

These specific attunements carry base concepts conveyed in the [[Draconic Kin Attunement]]
## Tier 1: Fledgling Hide

**NOTE:** Tier 1 active abilities award 1 attunement points per use

### Passives:

- **Thickening Scales:** Gain a natural armor bonus to AC equal to 2 * Attunement Tier - 1 (+1 AC at Tier 1).

- **Corrosive Vigor:** Gain a bonus on all Fortitude saves equal to your Attunement Tier (+1 at Tier 1).

### Actives:

- **Acidic Repulsion:** Immediate action. Completely dissolve and stop one incoming physical ranged attack (such as an arrow, bolt, or thrown weapon) before it strikes you.

- **Wide Guard:** Immediate action when an adjacent ally is hit by a physical attack. You intercept the attack, taking the full damage and any associated effects (bleed, poison, etc.) in place of the ally. You gain **DR 5/---** against this specific intercepted damage.

Attunement ☐☐☐☐

## Tier 2: Draconic Expansion

**NOTE:** Tier 2 active abilities Attune you to your claws faster, awarding 2 attunement points per use


### Passives:

- **Thickening Scales:** Retains formula (2 * Attunement Tier - 1).
Natural armor bonus increases to **+3**.

- **Corrosive Vigor:** Retains formula. Fortitude save bonus increases to **+2**.

- **Hardened Plates:** Gain DR equal to your Attunement Tier (**DR 2/---** at Tier 2). Stacks directly with Barbarian DR.

### Actives:

- **Acidic Repulsion:** Same as Tier 1.

- **Wide Guard:** Same as Tier 1.

- **Caustic Armor:** Swift action. For 1 round, any creature that hits you with a natural or non-reach melee weapon takes \$2\\text{d}6\$ Acid damage and must succeed on a Reflex save (\$\\text{DC } 10 + 1/2 \\text{ Character Level} + \\text{Con modifier}\$) or their weapon/natural attack takes half of that damage (ignoring hardness for manufactured weapons).

Attunement ☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐

## Tier 3: Sovereign Wyrm

**NOTE:** You are now fully attuned

### Passives:

- **Thickening Scales:** Retains formula (2 * Attunement Level - 1).
Natural armor bonus increases to **+5**.

- **Corrosive Vigor:** Retains formula. Fortitude save bonus increases to **+3**.

- **Hardened Plates:** Retains formula. DR increases to **DR 3/---** (stacks with Barbarian DR).

- **Black Wyrm Immunity:** Gain permanent **Acid Resistance 10**.

### Actives:

- **Acidic Repulsion:** Same as Tier 1.

- **Wide Guard:** Same as Tier 1.

- **Caustic Armor:** Same as Tier 2.

- **Mantle of the Black Wyrm:** Swift action. For 1 minute, you emit an aura of caustic fumes. Any creature that starts or ends its turn adjacent to you takes 2d6 Acid damage. This damage triggers a maximum of once per creature's turn.
