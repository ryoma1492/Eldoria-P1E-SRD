# Gold Dragon

An ancient gold dragon lying deep within the [[Savage Lands]].

It has been magically wounded by [[Malazar]]. A broken-off claw of the black dragon remains embedded in the wound, preventing it from ever fully closing.

The wound continually leaks raw [[Primal Energy]] into the surrounding wilderness. This leakage is the true origin of:
- the extraordinary abundance of [[Primals]]
- the increasingly powerful wildlife of the region
- primal plants
- the overwhelming magical vitality felt throughout the [[Savage Spire]] and the inland [[Savage Lands]]

---

## Nature of the Wound

The dragon is **not** failing to heal.  
It is healing *constantly*.

The embedded claw (saturated with [[Malazar]]’s residual magic) acts like a knife left in flesh. Every attempt by the dragon’s own power simply forms around the obstruction, so the wound continually reopens.

Only someone carrying fragments of [[Malazar]]’s own magical signature (currently the [[Heroes]] via [[Killian’s Curse]] and the draconic aspects) can safely grasp and remove the obstruction.

---

## Current State

- Immobile and suffering, but still immensely powerful
- Leaking primal energy at a rate that has reshaped the entire local ecology
- Aware of nearby events to a limited degree
- Not hostile to the [[Heroes]] once they demonstrate intent to help rather than exploit

---

## Resolution Path (Session 14+)

1. Remove [[Malazar]]’s claw (requires deeper attunement to the curse).  
2. Provide a living “seed” of unbound primal energy so the wound can finally begin to seal.  
3. The people of [[Savage Spire]] voluntarily surrender their own [[Primal Bonds]] to supply that seed.  
4. With the leak ended, the [[Savage Lands]] begin returning to balance.
