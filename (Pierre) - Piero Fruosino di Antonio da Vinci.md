# Pierre

**Role**: [[Leon]]’s father. Researcher. Accidental [[Baobab Tree Primal]]. Living archive of the [[Savage Lands]]’ greatest secret.  
**Current Status**: Rooted near the wounded [[Gold Dragon]]. Immobile. Fully conscious. Still researching.

---

## Core Identity

Pierre was (and remains) a scholar first.  
He never stopped asking questions, even after the world tried to kill him for it.

He is not a warrior, not a prophet, and not a tragic figure waiting to be rescued.  
He is a man who found the answer and now needs the only people who can act on it.

---

## Crossing the [[Fool’s Curtain]]

### The Research
Pierre spent years studying the physical and magical composition of the [[Fool’s Curtain]].

He discovered that a paste made from **[[Voidcap]] mushrooms** neutralizes the first two layers — the same principle later used by the [[Curtain  Splitter]] of [[Zarthas]].

### The Three Layers
1. **First Layer** – neutralized by [[Voidcap]] paste  
2. **Second Layer** – neutralized by [[Voidcap]] paste  
3. **Third Layer (The Malice Layer)**  
   - Pure, oppressive magical malice  
   - Instantly kills non-sentient life (plants, mindless organisms)  
   - For sentient beings, survival is tied directly to willpower  
   - Higher resolve = longer survival  

### The Crossing
Lacking volunteers, Pierre coated himself in [[Voidcap]] paste and attempted the crossing alone.

He maintained absolute, unyielding will.

It almost worked.

### The Stray Thought
Just before reaching the outer edge of the Malice Layer, one thought broke his focus:

**[[Leon]].**

The malice immediately began consuming him.

Because he was already near the outer edge, he survived — but the cost was catastrophic.

---

## Consequences of Survival

- Aged approximately **40–50 years** in seconds  
- Soul saturated with malignant magical energy  
- Magical **perforations** formed throughout his body  
- The malice continually tore outward through him (described as magical buckshot / birdshot exiting from within)  

Survival itself was considered miraculous.

---

## Arrival at [[Savage Spire]]

A salvage vessel recovered Pierre’s broken body from the water and brought him to the nearest settlement: **[[Savage Spire]]**.

The **[[The Mycelial Elder]]** diagnosed him and determined that only the life-shaping vitality of a **[[Primal Bond]]** could keep him alive.

Pierre was far too weak to survive bonding even the smallest local [[Primals]].

A hunting expedition was formed to carry him deeper into the [[Savage Lands]], toward a stronger source of primal energy.

---

## The [[Gold Dragon]] & the Accidental Bond

The expedition discovered an ancient **[[Gold Dragon]]** lying magically wounded, continually leaking raw [[Primal Energy]] into the surrounding wilderness.

This leakage is the true source of:
- The extraordinary abundance of [[Primals]]  
- Increasingly powerful wildlife  
- Primal plants  
- The overwhelming magical vitality of the region  

### How the Bond Happened
The hunting party intended to harvest a plant Primal and return to [[Savage Spire]] for a controlled bonding ritual (e.g. the [[Temple Water Ritual]].

Instead, Pierre’s magically porous, perforated body reacted violently with the dragon’s open wound.

The contact **forcibly triggered** a [[Primal Bond]] on the spot — no temple, no priest, no ritual.

Pierre bonded with a nearby **[[Baobab Tree Primal]]**.

---

## Current Physical State

The bond stabilized his life force, but rooted him permanently.

Because tree [[Primals]] change slowly, Pierre retained far more of his original self than most bonded individuals:

- Deep roots anchoring him in place  
- Scattered leaves emerging from his body  
- Patches of thick, knobby baobab bark across his skin  
- Clearly aged appearance (the 40–50 years he lost)  
- Otherwise **recognizable facial features**

A successful Perception check makes these details increasingly obvious the longer someone observes him.

He cannot travel.  
He cannot return to [[Savage Spire]].  
He is not dying.

---

## Personality & Behavior

Pierre never stopped being a researcher.

Even rooted.  
Even transformed.

He continues:
- Documenting observations  
- Asking questions  
- Developing theories  
- Trying to solve the mysteries in front of him  

He does **not** ask to be rescued.  
He asks for data.  
He asks for clarification.  
He asks whether the party has noticed certain patterns.

His greatest contribution to the campaign is not combat power.  
It is **understanding**.

---

## The Wound – Pierre’s Critical Insight

Pierre eventually reached the correct conclusion:

> The dragon is **not** failing to heal.  
> The dragon is healing *constantly*.  
> Something simply prevents the wound from ever closing.

**Analogy he uses**:  
The wound is like a knife left buried in flesh.  
Every attempt to heal merely forms around the obstruction.  
As long as the knife remains, the wound continually reopens.

The “knife” is a broken-off claw of [[Malazar]], still embedded and saturated with the black dragon’s residual magic.

---

## Why Pierre Cannot Heal the Wound

Pierre understands both the problem and the solution.

He lacks the necessary connection to [[Malazar]].

Only [[Malazar]]’s own magical signature can safely interact with the obstruction.

Ironically, the heroes now possess fragments of that signature through [[Killian’s Curse]] / the draconic aspects.

These fragments are the only means of grasping and removing the magical “knife.”

Doing so, however, requires the heroes to willingly embrace **additional** corruption and deepen their attunement to the curse.

Pierre has the knowledge.  
The heroes have the resonance.  

Only together can the wound finally be healed.

---

## After the Obstruction Is Removed

Once the claw/signature is extracted, the dragon immediately attempts to heal.

However, the wound has remained open for so long that it lacks sufficient unbound [[Primal Energy]] to begin sealing itself.

It needs a catalyst — a living “seed.”

The people of [[Savage Spire]] provide the answer voluntarily:

Those who accompany the heroes can surrender their own [[Primal Bonds]].  
Their bonded primal energies return to the dragon and become the initial seed patch from which the wound finally closes.

The participants survive.  
They simply become fully human once again.

To the people of the [[Savage Lands]] this is not loss of power.  
It is returning a sacred gift to its rightful source.

---

## Key Relationships

- **[[Leon]]**: The stray thought that nearly killed him. The reason he is still alive. The son he has not seen in years.  
- **[[Gold Dragon]]**: The source of the [[Primal Energy]] that saved him and the patient whose wound he has diagnosed.  
- **[[Malazar]]**: The architect of the wound and the reason only the heroes can finish what Pierre started.  
- **[[The Mycelial Elder]] / [[Savage Spire]]**: The people who refused to let him die and who will later offer the final sacrifice.  
- **[[Fool’s Curtain]]**: The barrier he crossed at terrible cost.  
- **[[Voidcap]]**: The key that allowed him to survive the first two layers.

- Speaks calmly, precisely, and with genuine curiosity.

- Never begs. Never dramatizes his condition.  
- Occasionally makes dry observations about how inconvenient it is to be a tree.
