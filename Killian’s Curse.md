# Killian’s Curse

The lingering draconic corruption that once threatened to fully transform [[Captain Killian]] into a vassal of [[Malazar]].

During the [[Temple Water Ritual]], the corruption was interrupted, divided, and redistributed among the [[Heroes]]. Rather than allowing a singular draconic possession to complete, the ritual fractured the essence into six conceptual aspects of draconic existence:

| Aspect   | Principle   |
|----------|-------------|
| Wings    | Freedom     |
| Claws    | Violence    |
| Scales   | Endurance   |
| Eyes     | Awareness   |
| Breath   | Power       |
| Horns    | Dominion    |

These are not gifts. They are organs of a larger being attempting to reassemble itself through mortal vessels.

Every use of the aspects strengthens [[Malazar]]’s lingering influence and deepens the bearer’s attunement to his magical signature.

---

## Narrative Importance (Session 14+)

The fragments of [[Malazar]]’s signature now carried by the [[Heroes]] are the **only** means by which the embedded claw can be safely grasped and removed from the [[Gold Dragon]]’s wound.

To do so, the party must willingly embrace further corruption and deepen their attunement.

This is the price of helping the dragon — and of finishing what [[(Pierre) - Piero Fruosino di Antonio da Vinci]] began.
