# Voidcap

A rare mushroom whose paste is the only known substance that neutralizes the first two layers of the [[Fool’s Curtain]].

Discovered through years of research by [[(Pierre) - Piero Fruosino di Antonio da Vinci]]. The same principle was later independently (or derivatively) applied by the [[Curtain  Splitter]] of [[Zarthas]].

---

## Properties

- Neutralizes the first and second layers of the [[Fool’s Curtain]] when applied as a paste.
- Does **not** protect against the third layer (The Malice Layer).
- Essential component in the functioning of [[Curtain  Splitter]].
- Extremely rare and difficult to cultivate outside specific conditions.

---

## Notable Uses

- [[(Pierre) - Piero Fruosino di Antonio da Vinci]] coated himself in Voidcap paste to attempt a full crossing of the Curtain.
- [[Zarthas]] uses refined Voidcap (or an equivalent process) inside their Splitter devices.
- [[Nibwick]] once consumed a large reserve of Voidcap, temporarily losing his ability to fly and turn invisible (Session 9).
