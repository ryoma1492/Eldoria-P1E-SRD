# Encounter: The Claw Emerges (CR 9)

**Timing**: Mid-session  
**Tone**: High threat, low HP, snappy (3–5 rounds)  
**Purpose**: First real interaction with the gold dragon’s wound. Removes one aggressive piece of Malazar’s lingering will without fully solving the problem.

## Setup

The party reaches the wounded [[adult-gold-dragon|Gold Dragon]].  
A long, jagged gash leaks raw primal energy (golden-green motes).  

[[Pierre]] (or investigation) has already concluded: something is still lodged in the wound.

**Trigger**  
Any serious attempt to examine, touch, heal, or magically interact with the gash causes the obstruction to react.

A wet tearing sound.  
A single enormous, razor-sharpened **black claw** — clearly broken from a much larger dragon — rips free of the wound and rises into the air.

## Malazar’s Severed Claw (CR 7)

*Large construct (evil)*

**Init** +7; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., blindsense 30 ft.; Perception +8

### Defense
**AC** 23, touch 12, flat-footed 20 (+3 Dex, +11 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 76 (9d10+27)  
**Fort** +3, **Ref** +6, **Will** +5  
**Defensive Abilities** hardness 12  
**Immune** construct traits, acid  
**Weaknesses** Malazar’s Signature

### Offense
**Speed** fly 50 ft. (perfect)  
**Melee** claw +17 (2d8+12/19–20 plus 2d6 acid and *reopen*)  
**Space** 10 ft.; **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** *reopen the wound*, *malice pulse*, Power Attack (–3/+6)

### Statistics
**Str** 26, **Dex** 16, **Con** —, **Int** 5, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 14  
**Base Atk** +9; **CMB** +18; **CMD** 31  
**Feats** Improved Critical (claw), Improved Initiative, Power Attack, Weapon Focus (claw), Vital Strike  
**Skills** Fly +15, Perception +8, Stealth +5  
**Languages** understands Draconic (cannot speak)

### Special Abilities

**Reopen the Wound (Su)**  
On a hit, target takes 1d6 persistent bleed.  
While within 60 ft. of the gold dragon’s wound, this bleed is harder to stop (Heal DC 20 or CL check DC 20).

**Malice Pulse (Su)**  
Once every 1d3 rounds as a swift action.  
15-ft. burst: 4d6 acid + sickened 1 round (Fort DC 17 half and negates sickened).  
Briefly intensifies the leak (narrative only).

**Wound-Tethered (Ex)**  
Cannot move more than 80 ft. from the wound.  
If forced farther, is yanked back adjacent to the wound at the start of its next turn.

**Malazar’s Signature (Su)**  
Against any creature carrying fragments of Malazar’s corruption (via Killian’s curse):  
+2 attack and damage, critical threat range becomes 18–20.

**Residual Malice (Su)**  
Fast healing 4 while the main wound remains open.  
Suppressed if the party makes meaningful progress toward beginning to close the wound.

## Tactics

- **Claw** prioritizes whoever is interacting with the wound.  
  Uses *malice pulse* early, then focuses Power Attack claw strikes.  
  Never leaves the wound area.

- **Overflow Beasts** flank and trip. They are short-lived pressure.

- If Savage Spire warriors are present, they fight with calm professionalism. Quiet comments only. One may simply observe: “The knife still has a pulse.”

---

## Aftermath

When the Claw is destroyed it dissolves into black motes that the leaking energy slowly burns away.  
That section of the wound visibly calms for a short time.

**This does not close the wound.**  
It only removes one active piece of Malazar’s will.  

The true obstruction remains deeper — a magical signature that only someone carrying Malazar’s own corruption can safely grasp and extract.

This sets up:
- The need for deeper attunement to the curse
- [[Pierre]]’s knowledge + the heroes’ resonance
- The eventual voluntary sacrifice of Primal Bonds by the Savage people
