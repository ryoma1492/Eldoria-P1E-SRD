# Encounter: The Claw Emerges (CR 9)

**Timing**: Mid-session  
**Tone**: High threat, low HP, snappy (3–5 rounds)  
**Purpose**: First real interaction with the gold dragon’s wound. Removes one aggressive piece of Malazar’s lingering will without fully solving the problem.

## Setup

The party reaches the wounded [[adult-gold-dragon|Gold Dragon]].  
A long, jagged gash leaks raw primal energy (golden-green motes).  

[[(Pierre) - Piero Fruosino di Antonio da Vinci|Pierre]] (or investigation) has already concluded: something is still lodged in the wound.

**Trigger**  
Any serious attempt to examine, touch, heal, or magically interact with the gash causes the obstruction to react.

A wet tearing sound.  
A single enormous, razor-sharpened **black claw** — clearly broken from a much larger dragon — rips free of the wound and rises into the air.

---

## Creatures

- 1 × [[Malazar’s Severed Claw]] (CR 7)
- 2 × [[Overgrown Primal Beast]] (CR 4) — arrive at the start of round 2 (or already nearby)

---


---

## Tactics

- **Claw** prioritizes whoever is interacting with the wound.  
  Uses *malice pulse* early, then focuses Power Attack claw strikes.  
  Never leaves the wound area.

- **Overflow Beasts** flank and trip. They are short-lived pressure.

- If Savage Spire warriors are present, they fight with calm professionalism. Quiet comments only. One may simply observe: “The knife still has a pulse.”

---

## Aftermath

When the Claw is destroyed it dissolves into black motes that the leaking energy slowly burns away.  
That section of the wound visibly calms for a short time.

**This does not close the wound.**  
It only removes one active piece of Malazar’s will.  

The true obstruction remains deeper — a magical signature that only someone carrying Malazar’s own corruption can safely grasp and extract.

This sets up:
- The need for deeper attunement to the curse
- [[(Pierre) - Piero Fruosino di Antonio da Vinci]]’s knowledge + the heroes’ resonance
- The eventual voluntary sacrifice of Primal Bonds by the Savage people
