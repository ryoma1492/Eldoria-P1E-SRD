# Primal Monsters – Full Set

These are the amplified “Primal” versions of common animals for the Savage Lands.  
Each is treated as a **magical beast** (augmented animal) with roughly **double to triple** the physical ability scores, natural armor, and damage of its mundane counterpart.

### Notes for Use
- All Primals are aggressive when their territory or the leak is disturbed.
- They fight to the death more often than normal animals.
- Feel free to mix and match (e.g., a pack of Primal Wolves + a Primal Lion as a “pride” encounter).
