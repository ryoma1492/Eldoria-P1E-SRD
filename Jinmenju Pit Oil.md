**Source:** Custom (Based on Jinmenju Ecology)
**Price** 1,200 gp; Weight —
**Type** ingested or injury; Save Fortitude DC 22
**Onset** — (injury), 1 minute (ingested); Frequency 1/round for 6 rounds
**Effect** 1d3 Wisdom damage and confused for 1 round
**Cure** 2 consecutive saves
**Description**
Extracted from the large pit of a female jinmenju's fruit with a successful DC 20 Craft (alchemy) or Survival check, this pungent oil mimics the disorienting chemical properties of the male tree's head-fruit. Unextracted pits remain viable for months if kept dry, though exposed oil spoils and loses potency after 1 hour of air exposure.
