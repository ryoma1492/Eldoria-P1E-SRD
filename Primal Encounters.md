# Primal Encounters (CR ≤ 7)

Mid-game encounters for the Savage Lands. All stay at or under CR 7. Resting between fights is assumed and acceptable.

---

## 1. Jungle Path Ambush (CR 5)

**Creatures**
- 2 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Wolf (CR 3)|Primal Wolf]]
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Fox (CR 1)|Primal Fox]]

**Tactics**  
Wolves flank and trip. Fox harasses the back line / casters. Classic pack tactics.

---

## 2. Canopy Harassment (CR 4–5)

**Creatures**
- 2 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Hawk (CR 2)|Primal Hawk]]
- 2 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Cockatoo (CR 1)|Primal Cockatoo]]
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Dragonfly (CR 1)|Primal Dragonfly]]

**Tactics**  
Flyers dive-bomb and stay mobile. Easy to kite if the party has ranged options; annoying if they don’t.

---

## 3. River Crossing (CR 4)

**Creatures**
- 2 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Trout (CR 1)|Primal Trout]]
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Turtle (CR 3)|Primal Turtle]]

**Tactics**  
Trout try to grab and drag into deeper water. Turtle anchors the fight and soaks damage with its shell.

---

## 4. Undergrowth Ambush (CR 5–6)

**Creatures**
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Leopard (CR 5)|Primal Leopard]]
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Chameleon (CR 2)|Primal Chameleon]] *or* 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Iguana (CR 2)|Primal Iguana]]

**Tactics**  
Leopard pounces the softest target. Chameleon/Iguana stays hidden and supports with tongue grab or flanking.

---

## 5. Ant Colony Edge (CR 6)

**Creatures**
- 4 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Ant (CR 2)|Primal Ant]]

**Tactics**  
Simple and relentless. They climb walls/trees freely and focus fire. Numbers are the threat.

---

## 6. Pride Territory (CR 7)

**Creatures**
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Lion (CR 6)|Primal Lion]]
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Wolf (CR 3)|Primal Wolf]] *or* 2 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Fox (CR 1)|Primal Fox]]

**Tactics**  
Lion is the star — pounce + rake. Support creatures keep pressure on anyone trying to kite or heal.

---

## 7. Lightning Bug Hollow (CR 4)

**Creatures**
- 4 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Lightning Bug|Primal Lightning Bug]]

**Tactics**  
Bugs light up the area and deliver small electric bites. Good atmospheric fight.

---

## 8. Heavyweight Pair (CR 7)

**Creatures**
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Bear (CR 6)|Primal Bear]]
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Cobra (CR 3)|Primal Cobra]]

**Tactics**  
Bear grapples and mauls. Cobra stays mobile and delivers poison. Classic “tank + poison” duo.

---

## 9. Tall Grass Stalkers (CR 5–6)

**Creatures**
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Giraffe (CR 5)|Primal Giraffe]]
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Leopard (CR 5)|Primal Leopard]] → *use only one of each, or drop the leopard for a lighter fight*

**Simpler version (CR 5)**: Just the Giraffe + 1 [[Primal Monsters#Primal Fox (CR 1)|Primal Fox]] or [[Primal Monsters#Primal Hawk (CR 2)|Primal Hawk]].

**Tactics**  
Giraffe uses reach and trampling presence. Leopard (if present) flanks.


____

## 10. Armored Foragers (CR 4)

**Creatures**
- 3 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Armadillo|Primal Armadillo]]
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Fox (CR 1)|Primal Fox]]

**Tactics**  
The armadillos form a slow, durable front line and ball up when focused. The fox darts around the edges, harassing anyone who tries to ignore the rolling tanks. Surprisingly sticky for such small creatures.

---

## 11. Shell and Scale (CR 5)

**Creatures**
- 2 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Armadillo|Primal Armadillo]]
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Iguana (CR 2)|Primal Iguana]]
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Cobra (CR 3)|Primal Cobra]]

**Tactics**  
Armadillos soak damage and protect the more dangerous pair. The iguana climbs and flanks while the cobra delivers poison. Cute tanks + actual threats.

---

## 12. Shell and Fang (CR 5–6)

**Creatures**
- 2 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Cobra (CR 3)|Primal Cobra]]
- 1 × [[Primal Monsters#Primal Turtle (CR 3)|Primal Turtle]]

**Tactics**  
The turtle acts as a durable front-line sponge (high AC + shell). The two cobras stay mobile, flank, and deliver poison. Classic “tank + dual strikers” setup.
