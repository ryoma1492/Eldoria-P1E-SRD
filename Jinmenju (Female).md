### DESCRIPTION

The female **jinmenju** is an immobile, non-predatory plant that closely resembles a weeping willow with dark, thick bark and long, downward-drooping branches. When bearing fruit, the heavy produce weighs down these sweeping branches until they brush low against the ground. The fruit itself resembles a large, sweet-smelling stone fruit—similar to a mango—housing a massive, oil-rich pit.

While the female plant lacks the predatory aggression of the male, it continuously releases volatile organic pheromones into the surrounding soil and air. A male jinmenju rooted within a 2-mile radius absorbs these airborne and soil chemicals, which enables the male to grow its signature enticing head-fruits. If separated from a female tree for more than 48 hours, a male's existing head-fruits wither and drop off, and no new fruit can grow.

A single female jinmenju produces 1d4+2 fruits per week during its 12-week growing season each year. The outer flesh is harmless, rich, and unpleasant.  They are sought, however, for their pits.   The large central pit contains a volatile oil ( [[Jinmenju Pit Oil]] )  that can be harvested with a **DC 20 Craft (alchemy)** or **Survival** check per pit and stored in an airtight vessel. Each single pit can only produce one application of poison (after successfully coating the weapon or ammo).  The extracted oil spoils after 2 hours of air exposure (even on weapons), though intact pits remain viable for months if stored in low humidity.
