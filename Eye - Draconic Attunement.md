A portion of the power of [[Killian’s Curse|Killian's Curse]] shared among the [[Heroes]] outside the [[Fool’s Curtain|Fool's Curtain]]

These specific attunements carry base concepts conveyed in the [[Draconic Kin Attunement]]
## Tier 1: Fledgling Sight

### Passives:

- **Terrifying Aura:** Gain a bonus on Intimidate and Perception checks equal to 2 * Attunement Tier (+2 at Tier 1).

- **Putrifying Gaze:** Concentrating your draconic glare on non-magical food or drink for 1 full round instantly spoils, rots, or mutates it into a toxic version. (Drinking or eating causes 1d4 - 1 damage).

### Actives:

- **Baleful Focus:** Standard action (or usable as part of starting or maintaining a Bardic Performance). Target one creature within 60 feet. It takes a penalty equal to 2 * Attunement Tier on saving throws against your Bardic Performances and any spells cast by you or your allies for 1 minute. Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 Character Level + Cha modifier) negates the spell save penalty.

- **Enrapturing Gaze:** Swift action. One creature within 30 ft must succeed on a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 Character Level + Cha modifier) or lock eyes with you for 2 * Attunement Tier rounds. While staring, the target takes a penalty to Reflex and Will saves equal to Attunement Tier, and a -(5 * Attunement Tier) penalty to Perception checks.

## Tier 2: Draconic Expansion

### Passives:

- **Terrifying Aura:** Retains formula: 2 * Attunement Tier. Bonus on Intimidate and Perception checks increases to +4.

- **Putrifying Gaze:** Retains Tier 1 effects. In addition, concentrating your gaze for 1 full round on any swarm of living creatures within 30 feet corrupts them into a toxic, acid-dripping, deformed Malazarian Swarm for 1 minute. (Malazarian animals cause 1d4 - 1 damage per round to hold, and 2d4 damage to consume).

- **Soul-splitting Stare:** Gain a bonus on Sense Motive checks equal to 5 * Attunement Tier (+10 at Tier 2).

### Actives:

- **Baleful Focus:** Same as Tier 1.

- **Enrapturing Gaze:** Same as Tier 1.

- **Exertion of Draconic Will:** Move action. Harmonize a second bardic performance into your current active performance (paying the additional round cost per round) as long as the second performance affects enemy targets (e.g., Distraction, Fascinate, Suggestion, Dirge of Doom). Feats affecting Bardic Performance apply to both active performances.

## Tier 3: Sovereign Wyrm

**NOTE:** You are now fully attuned

### Passives:

- **Terrifying Aura:** Retains formula: 2 * Attunement Tier. Bonus on Intimidate and Perception checks increases to +6.

- **Putrifying Gaze:** Retains Tier 1 and Tier 2 effects.

- **Soul-splitting Stare:** Retains formula: 5 * Attunement Tier.
Bonus on Sense Motive checks increases to +15.

- **Gaze of Truth:** Permanently see through all illusions and invisibility within 30 feet, as if under a continuous See Invisibility spell.

- **Corrosive Presence:** Permanent Acid Resistance 5.

### Actives:

- **Baleful Focus:** Same as Tier 1.

- **Enrapturing Gaze:** Same as Tier 1.

- **Exertion of Draconic Will:** Same as Tier 2.

- **Wyrm's Majesty:** Standard action, or usable as part of starting or maintaining a Bardic Performance. All enemies within 30 feet (or all enemies currently hearing your Bardic Performance within 60 feet) must succeed on a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 Character Level + Cha modifier) or become Frightened for 1d4 rounds (Shaken for 1 round on a successful save).
