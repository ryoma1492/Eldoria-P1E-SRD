A portion of the power of [[Killian’s Curse|Killian's Curse]] shared among the [[Heroes]] outside the [[Fool’s Curtain|Fool's Curtain]]

These specific attunements carry base concepts conveyed in the [[Draconic Kin Attunement]]
## Tier 1: Fledgling Wings

*Your back bears the budding, leathery weight of black dragon wings. They are flightless and mostly downy, but they instinctively respond to your body's physical movements.*

### Passives

- **Draconic Stride:** You gain a permanent **+5 ft bonus to your base land speed**.

- **Cushioned Descent:** The wings naturally flare out to catch the air when you drop. You reduce all falling damage by **1d6** (treating falls as if they were 10 feet shorter).

- **Draconic Balance:** The wings act as a natural counterweight to your movements. You gain a permanent **+2 bonus on Acrobatics checks**.

### Actives (Cost: 1 Daily Use)

- **Draconic Shroud:** As a **Swift Action** on your turn, you wrap the dark, webbed wings around yourself to completely muddy your silhouette. You gain a **+5 bonus on Stealth checks** for 1 round.

- **Winged Obfuscation (Reaction):** As an **Immediate Action** when targeted by an attack, you flare your wings to disrupt your silhouette and throw off your attacker's aim.
 - **No Concealment:** You gain **partial concealment** (20% miss chance) against that specific attack.
 - **Partial Concealment:** It upgrades to **total concealment** (50% miss chance) against that specific attack.
 - **Total Concealment:** You **automatically enter Stealth** without needing to spend an action or roll a check, completely hiding your location from that attacker.
 - *Restriction:* This provides no offensive advantage on the triggering attack and cannot be used to instantly cause a Sneak Attack on the enemy's turn.

## Tier 2: Draconic Expansion

*The wings grow physically larger, and thick, midnight-black scales begin to harden across the limbs. Your physical control over them tightens as the curse disperses into raw power. *

**NOTE:** Tier 2 active abilities Attune you to your wings faster, awarding 2 attunement points per use

### Passives

- **Draconic Stride:** You retain your permanent **+5 ft bonus to your base land speed**.

- **Parachute Canopy:** The expanding surface area catches the air with immense force. You reduce all falling damage by **2d6** (treating falls as if they were 20 feet shorter).

- **Draconic Balance:** Your center of gravity becomes incredibly stable. You gain a permanent **+4 bonus on Acrobatics checks**.

### Actives (Cost: 1 Daily Use)

- **Draconic Shroud:** Functions exactly as Tier 1 (+5 bonus on Stealth checks for 1 round).

- **Winged Obfuscation (Reaction):** Functions exactly as Tier 1.

- **Buffeting Escape:** As a **Move Action**, you violently beat your growing wings to create a blinding burst of foul wind and acrid swamp dust while leaping backward. Make a **Dirty Trick combat maneuver** against a single adjacent enemy's **CMD**, rolling 1d20 + your Stealth modifier + 2.
 - **Success:** The target is blinded for 1 round.
 - **Movement:** Whether the maneuver succeeds or fails against their CMD, you can immediately move up to 10 feet away without provoking an attack of opportunity from that specific enemy.

Attunement ☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐

## Tier 3: Sovereign Wyrm

*The wings reach their absolute mature span, rippling with muscle and coated in impenetrable, acid-scarred scales. The foreign consciousness of the dragon is fully conquered, giving you total mastery over its ultimate forms.*

**NOTE:** you are fully attuned to your wings

### Passives

- **Draconic Stride:** You retain your permanent **+5 ft bonus to your base land speed**.

- **True Glide:** The wings are large enough to fully halt your terminal velocity. You reduce all falling damage by **3d6** (treating falls as if they were 30 feet shorter).

- **Draconic Balance:** You move across narrow surfaces with supernatural grace. You gain a permanent **+6 bonus on Acrobatics checks**.

- **Sovereign Scale:** The hardened black dragon hide fully matures.
You gain permanent **Acid Resistance 5**.

### Actives (Cost: 1 Daily Use)

- **Draconic Shroud:** Functions exactly as Tier 1 (+5 bonus on Stealth checks for 1 round).

- **Winged Obfuscation (Reaction):** Functions exactly as Tier 1.

- **Buffeting Escape:** Functions exactly as Tier 2.

- **Vortex Blast:** As a **Standard Action**, you snap your massive dragon wings forward with explosive force, unleashing a violent hurricane gale in a **15-foot cone** originating from you. Every creature caught in the cone must succeed on a **DC 20 Reflex save** or be knocked **Prone** by the crushing wind pressure.

- **Temporary Flight:** As a **Swift Action**, you fully override the physical limits of your halfling frame and force the wings into a powerful, rhythmic beat. You gain a **Fly speed of 40 feet (Good maneuverability)** for 1 minute.
