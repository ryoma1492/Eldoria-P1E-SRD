Rules for split cursed [#attunement]:

- **Daily Use Pool:** You have a shared pool of daily uses for your activated abilities equal to your **Dexterity modifier + your current Attunement Tier**.

- **The Token System:** Every time you use an **Active Ability**, you gain **1+ Attunement Token** on your tracker. These tokens represent your growing synchronization with the draconic graft and are used to unlock the next Tier.

- **Lore Restriction:** Because these draconic parts were originally born of a curse and belonged to another entity, their passive physical structures are always active, but their supernatural draconic features must be consciously forced awake using your daily pool.

- **Lingering Curse**: Each time you use an active ability (and gain requisite Attunement) roll a 3d6, if the roll is higher than your character level + Tier attunement, draw a card from the "Cursed Deck of Draconic Will" and keep it in effect until it is discarded
