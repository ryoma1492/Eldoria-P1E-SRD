A portion of the power of [[Killian’s Curse|Killian's Curse]] shared among the [[Heroes]] outside the [[Fool’s Curtain|Fool's Curtain]]

These specific attunements carry base concepts conveyed in the [[Draconic Kin Attunement]]
## Tier 1: Fledgling Claws

*The human flesh of your hands begins to harden and split, pushing out black, glistening dragon scales. Your fingers elongate into sharp talons that weep a faint, acrid sizzle of acid.*

**NOTE:** Tier 1 active abilities award 1 attunement points per use

### Passives (2 Total)

1.  **Caustic Grip:** You gain a permanent **+2 * Attunement Tier bonus to your CMD against Disarm attempts** made to strip a weapon from your clawed hand.

2.  **Corrosive Coated:** You deal a permanent **+2 * Attunement Tier Acid damage** on all successful attacks made with your equipped melee weapon.

### Actives (1 Total - Costs 1 Daily Use)

1.  **Overcharged Spellstrike:** As a **Free Action** when you cast a spell through your Spellstrike feature, you calculate the spell's damage and effects as if your caster level were higher for that casting, equal to **2 * Attunement Tier - 1** (+1 CL at Tier 1).

## Tier 2: Draconic Expansion

*The scales crawl further up your forearms, clashing harshly with your human skin. The acid sweating from your claws begins to hum with raw, ancient magical resonance.*

**NOTE:** Tier 2 active abilities Attune you to your claws faster, awarding 2 attunement points per use


### Passives (3 Total)

1.  **Caustic Grip:** Functions exactly as Tier 1 (+4 CMD vs Disarm).

2.  **Corrosive Coated:** Functions exactly as Tier 1 (+4 Acid damage).

3.  **Arcane Acidic Bond:** Your claws permanently memorize the dragon's signature spell. You permanently add ***Melf's Acid Arrow*** to your Magus spell list and your list of spells known, making it available for your Spellstrike feature.

### Actives (3 Total - Costs 1 Daily Use)

1.  **Overcharged Spellstrike:** Functions exactly as Tier 1 (+3 CL at Tier 2).

2.  **Reach Spellstrike:** You can cast any ranged touch attack spell from your pool as if it were a melee touch attack spell for the purpose of using your Spellstrike feature.

3.  **Acidic Backlash (Reaction):** As an **Immediate Action** when you are struck by a melee attack, you snap your claw outward to spray burning, defensive acid. The attacker must succeed on a Reflex save (DC 10 + 1/2 Character Level + Dex) or take **1d6 Acid damage**.

## Tier 3: Sovereign Wyrm

*The black scales fully bond with your arms, hardening into an impenetrable, acid-scarred chitin. Your human hands have been completely replaced by the predatory talons of a sovereign dragon.*

**NOTE:** You are now fully attuned

### Passives (4 Total)

1.  **Caustic Grip:** Functions exactly as Tier 1 (+6 CMD vs Disarm).

2.  **Corrosive Coated:** Functions exactly as Tier 1 (+6 Acid damage).

3.  **Sovereign Skin:** You gain permanent **Acid Resistance 5**.
 4.  **Gain the Spell Like Ability Draconic Caustic Blood**

**Draconic Caustic Blood: 3/day:** The transformation reaches your veins. You gain the passive benefits of the ***Caustic Blood** spell (effect lasts 1 round/level). Whenever you take piercing or slashing damage, your acid-infused human blood sprays out, automatically dealing 1d6 acid damage per caster level to the attacker. (see )

### Actives (3 Total - Costs 1 Daily Use)

1.  **Overcharged Spellstrike:** Functions exactly as Tier 1 (+5 CL at Tier 3).

2.  **Reach Spellstrike:** Functions exactly as Tier 2.

3.  **Corrosive Passante:** As a **Full-Round Action**, you can expend 1 use from your pool and Attunement usage pool to channel a spell via Spellstrike an 180 degree arc (3 hexes). swing your weapon and make a single melee attack roll, then compare it against the AC of **all affected enemies**. Any target hit takes weapon damage, your passive acid damage, and suffers the full effects of the delivered spell as from Spellstrike.
